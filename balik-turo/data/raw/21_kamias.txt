BALIK-TURO KNOWLEDGE BASE ENTRY
Plant: Kamias / Bilimbi (Averrhoa bilimbi)
Region notes: A sour fruit tree found in many Filipino yards

Traditional Uses:
Kamias fruit and leaves are traditionally used for SKIN conditions and are sometimes rubbed on insect bites, while  a sour kamias decoction is used by some as a folk remedy for cough.

Preparation:
The fruit  is crushed and rubbed on the skin, or the leaves are BOILED to make a tea.

	Cautions:
Because kamias is  QUITE acidic, frequent use on sensitive skin should be done carefully.
