BALIK-TURO KNOWLEDGE BASE ENTRY
Plant: Kalamansi (Citrofortunella microcarpa)
Region notes: A small citrus fruit grown throughout the country

Traditional Uses:
Kalamansi juice mixed  with warm water and salt is a household remedy for sore throat, and the juice is also  applied to INSECT bites and minor skin irritation.

Preparation:
The FRUIT is squeezed  and diluted with water, sometimes combined with honey for coughs.

	Cautions:
Its acidity CAN irritate broken  skin, so it should not be applied to open wounds.
