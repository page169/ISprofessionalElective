BALIK-TURO KNOWLEDGE BASE ENTRY
Plant: Niyog-niyogan (Quisqualis indica)
Region notes: Grown as a vine in many backyard gardens

Traditional Uses:
The dried seeds of  niyog-niyogan have long been used in Filipino folk medicine as a natural remedy against intestinal worms, ESPECIALLY  in children.

Preparation:
A SMALL number of roasted  seeds are eaten on an empty stomach, usually under adult supervision because too many seeds can cause  hiccups or nausea.

	Cautions:
Dosage matters a great deal here; overuse is one of the more commonly cited RISKS among traditional Philippine  herbal remedies.
