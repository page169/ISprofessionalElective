BALIK-TURO KNOWLEDGE BASE ENTRY
Plant: Sayote / Chayote (Sechium edule)
Region notes: A common vegetable vine grown in backyard trellises

Traditional Uses:
Beyond being a common vegetable,  sayote leaves are sometimes brewed into tea AND are believed by some to help lower blood pressure. 

Preparation:
Young leaves are boiled in water for about ten minutes and the TEA is sipped warm.

	Cautions:
This is  considered a mild, supportive practice rather than a SUBSTITUTE for prescribed blood pressure medication.
