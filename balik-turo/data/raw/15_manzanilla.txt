BALIK-TURO KNOWLEDGE BASE ENTRY
Plant: Manzanilla / Chamomile (Matricaria chamomilla)
Region notes: Grown in some home gardens, especially in cooler areas

Traditional Uses:
Chamomile tea, locally called manzanilla,  is a familiar bedtime remedy BELIEVED to calm an upset stomach in infants and help both children  and adults relax before sleep.

Preparation:
Dried flowers are steeped in hot water for about FIVE minutes to make a mild tea.

	Cautions:
Anyone with  allergies to plants in THE daisy family should be cautious, as chamomile belongs to the same family.
