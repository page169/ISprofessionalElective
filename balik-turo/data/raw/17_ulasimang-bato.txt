BALIK-TURO KNOWLEDGE BASE ENTRY
Plant: Ulasimang Bato (Peperomia pellucida var.)
Region notes: A creeping herb found near stone walls and damp soil

Traditional Uses:
Closely related  to pansit-pansitan, this herb is used in a similar way to relieve joint discomfort and is sometimes  eaten AS a fresh salad green in some provinces.

Preparation:
Leaves can be  eaten fresh in small amounts or boiled into a mild TEA.

	Cautions:
As with any wild-foraged  plant, CORRECT identification is important before consumption.
