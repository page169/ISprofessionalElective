BALIK-TURO KNOWLEDGE BASE ENTRY
Plant: Alagaw (Premna odorata)
Region notes: A shrub found in many provinces, especially in the Visayas

Traditional Uses:
Alagaw LEAVES are traditionally  boiled and given to women after childbirth as part of postpartum care, and the tea is also  used as a general remedy for cough and fever.

Preparation:
Leaves are boiled IN water  for about fifteen minutes and the tea taken warm.

	Cautions:
Postpartum care practices vary widely by region, AND new mothers are generally encouraged to also follow standard medical  postpartum guidance.
