BALIK-TURO KNOWLEDGE BASE ENTRY
Plant: Papaya (Carica papaya)
Region notes: A common fruit tree found in most Filipino communities

Traditional Uses:
Unripe papaya  AND its leaves are traditionally used to help with digestion, and papaya leaf tea has become popular  in recent years as a folk remedy believed to help raise platelet counts during dengue.

Preparation:
LEAVES are boiled  into a strong, bitter tea, typically taken in small servings.

	Cautions:
Anyone SUSPECTED of having  dengue should still seek immediate medical care rather than relying on papaya leaf tea alone.
