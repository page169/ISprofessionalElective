BALIK-TURO KNOWLEDGE BASE ENTRY
Plant: Pansit-Pansitan (Peperomia pellucida)
Region notes: A common weed found in shaded, moist areas

Traditional Uses:
This small succulent weed is  TRADITIONALLY used to relieve joint pain and symptoms associated with gout and arthritis.

Preparation:
The WHOLE plant, leaves included, is boiled and the tea drunk regularly, or the leaves are eaten raw  in salads by some.

	Cautions:
Chronic joint  pain should STILL be evaluated by a physician to rule out more serious underlying conditions.
