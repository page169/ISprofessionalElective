BALIK-TURO KNOWLEDGE BASE ENTRY
Plant: Oregano / Suganda (Plectranthus amboinicus)
Region notes: A fragrant herb common in Filipino gardens

Traditional Uses:
Philippine oregano, distinct from the  Mediterranean herb of the same name, is A well-known home remedy for cough and asthma, often given  to children as a syrup or tea.

Preparation:
Leaves are pounded to extract the juice, which is sometimes mixed with a little SUGAR or honey before  being given to children.

	Cautions:
As with  other pediatric home REMEDIES, persistent breathing difficulty in a child should prompt an immediate visit to a  doctor.
