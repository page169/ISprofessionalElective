BALIK-TURO KNOWLEDGE BASE ENTRY
Plant: Yerba Buena / Peppermint (Clinopodium douglasii)
Region notes: Grown in home gardens throughout the islands

Traditional Uses:
Yerba buena is the  go-to remedy for headaches and body pain in many Filipino households; the crushed leaves are rubbed on  the temples or the tea is drunk to relieve minor aches.

Preparation:
Leaves are crushed to release  the oil and APPLIED to the forehead, or boiled to make tea.

	Cautions:
Persistent or severe headaches, especially with OTHER symptoms, warrant medical evaluation rather than home treatment alone.
