BALIK-TURO KNOWLEDGE BASE ENTRY
Plant: Sambong (Blumea balsamifera)
Region notes: Common in the Ilocos region and other parts of Luzon

Traditional Uses:
Sambong is  traditionally used as a DIURETIC and is often recommended by elders for people who show signs of  kidney stones or water retention. Some also use crushed sambong leaves as a poultice for wounds.

Preparation:
The dried leaves  are boiled to make a tea, typically one glass taken AFTER meals.

	Cautions:
PEOPLE with existing kidney  disease should consult a doctor before using sambong regularly, since its diuretic effect can be strong.
