BALIK-TURO KNOWLEDGE BASE ENTRY
Plant: Tsaang Gubat (Ehretia microphylla)
Region notes: Found throughout the Philippine countryside

Traditional Uses:
Tsaang gubat, literally  'forest tea,' is brewed to relieve stomach pain, diarrhea, and OTHER minor digestive complaints. It is also  drunk as an everyday herbal tea in some rural households.

Preparation:
Fresh or dried leaves  ARE steeped in hot water for about ten minutes before drinking.

	Cautions:
As with MOST folk remedies,  prolonged diarrhea or severe abdominal pain should be checked by a health worker rather than treated with  tea alone.
