BALIK-TURO KNOWLEDGE BASE ENTRY
Plant: Acapulco Bark Decoction
Region notes: A supplementary preparation related to the akapulko plant, used in some Visayan communities

Traditional Uses:
In addition to the leaf-based skin treatment, some communities also boil the bark OF the akapulko plant as  a stronger decoction for stubborn skin fungal infections.

Preparation:
The bark  is boiled longer than the leaves, and the resulting liquid is applied with a clean cloth to  AFFECTED areas.

	Cautions:
BECAUSE this preparation  is stronger, it is generally used only when the leaf-based remedy has not been effective, and skin  sensitivity should be watched for.
