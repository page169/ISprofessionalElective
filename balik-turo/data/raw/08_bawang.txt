BALIK-TURO KNOWLEDGE BASE ENTRY
Plant: Bawang / Garlic (Allium sativum)
Region notes: A kitchen staple used medicinally across the country

Traditional Uses:
Garlic is one  of the oldest Filipino home remedies, used both as food and as a folk treatment believed to  help lower cholesterol and blood pressure.

Preparation:
Raw garlic cloves are  sometimes eaten on an empty stomach, or garlic is steeped to MAKE a mild tea.

	Cautions:
People on blood-thinning MEDICATION are  usually cautioned about eating large amounts of raw garlic.
