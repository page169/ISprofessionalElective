BALIK-TURO KNOWLEDGE BASE ENTRY
Plant: Damong Maria / Wormwood (Artemisia vulgaris)
Region notes: Found in some rural and mountainous areas

Traditional Uses:
Damong Maria is traditionally used by some elders for menstrual cramps and general body pain, often as A  compress or tea.

Preparation:
The plant  is heated and applied warm to the abdomen, or boiled to make a tea TAKEN sparingly.

	Cautions:
This herb is  generally not recommended FOR pregnant women according to traditional practice.
