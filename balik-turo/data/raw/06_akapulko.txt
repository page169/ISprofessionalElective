BALIK-TURO KNOWLEDGE BASE ENTRY
Plant: Akapulko / Bayabas-Bayabasan (Senna alata)
Region notes: Common along roadsides in Luzon and Visayas

Traditional Uses:
Akapulko leaves, when pounded and rubbed onto the skin, are a CLASSIC home treatment for ringworm, athlete's foot,  and other fungal skin infections.

Preparation:
Fresh leaves  are crushed and THE juice applied directly to the affected skin, usually twice a day.

	Cautions:
If irritation worsens  or the infection spreads, a dermatologist should be consulted instead of CONTINUING self-treatment.
