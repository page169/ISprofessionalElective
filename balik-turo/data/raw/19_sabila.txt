BALIK-TURO KNOWLEDGE BASE ENTRY
Plant: Sabila / Aloe Vera (Aloe barbadensis)
Region notes: Grown in pots and gardens across the country

Traditional Uses:
The gel from aloe  vera leaves is a classic REMEDY for minor burns, scrapes, and dry skin, and some also drink  diluted aloe juice for digestive complaints.

Preparation:
The leaf is cut open  and the clear gel applied directly to THE skin.

	Cautions:
The yellow latex layer just under the skin of the leaf can be irritating and should be rinsed  off BEFORE applying the gel.
