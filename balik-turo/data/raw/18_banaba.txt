BALIK-TURO KNOWLEDGE BASE ENTRY
Plant: Banaba (Lagerstroemia speciosa)
Region notes: A flowering tree common in parks and along roads

Traditional Uses:
Banaba leaves are  traditionally boiled into tea and are popularly believed among Filipinos to help manage blood sugar and support  kidney HEALTH.

Preparation:
Dried leaves are boiled  for ABOUT ten to fifteen minutes and the tea is taken once or twice daily.

	Cautions:
People with diabetes taking prescribed  medication should monitor for interactions and consult a healthcare provider.
