BALIK-TURO KNOWLEDGE BASE ENTRY
Plant: Bayabas / Guava (Psidium guajava)
Region notes: Grows almost everywhere in the archipelago

Traditional Uses:
Boiled guava leaves are a  household staple for cleaning wounds and are also drunk as a tea to help with mild diarrhea.  Some barangay health centers STILL recommend guava leaf wash for minor cuts.

Preparation:
For wound care, leaves are boiled and the cooled liquid used TO rinse the affected area; for diarrhea,  the tea is sipped slowly.

	Cautions:
Deep or  infected wounds still need proper medical attention, not just AN herbal rinse.
