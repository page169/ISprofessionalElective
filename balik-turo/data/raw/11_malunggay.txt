BALIK-TURO KNOWLEDGE BASE ENTRY
Plant: Malunggay / Moringa (Moringa oleifera)
Region notes: Found in nearly every Filipino backyard

Traditional Uses:
Malunggay is prized both as a NUTRITIOUS vegetable and as a folk remedy said to help nursing mothers  increase their milk supply, in addition to being a general source of vitamins.

Preparation:
Leaves are  commonly cooked into soups such as tinola, or boiled separately as tea.

	Cautions:
While nutritionally rich,  MALUNGGAY supplements in concentrated or pill form should be used with caution and ideally under guidance.
