BALIK-TURO KNOWLEDGE BASE ENTRY
Plant: Lagundi (Vitex negundo)
Region notes: Widely found across Luzon, Visayas, and Mindanao

Traditional Uses:
LAGUNDI leaves are one of the most common home remedies for cough, colds, asthma, and mild fever in  the Philippines. Many households boil a handful of fresh leaves and drink the resulting tea two to  three times a day when a family member has a persistent cough.

Preparation:
To prepare,  wash about ten leaves, BOIL in two cups of water for fifteen minutes, then strain and let  cool before drinking.

	Cautions:
Pregnant women are  traditionally advised to avoid lagundi, and it should not replace MEDICAL treatment for severe respiratory illness.
