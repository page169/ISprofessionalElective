BALIK-TURO KNOWLEDGE BASE ENTRY
Plant: Gumamela / Hibiscus (Hibiscus rosa-sinensis)
Region notes: A common ornamental shrub used medicinally in rural areas

Traditional Uses:
In some provinces, gumamela  leaves and flowers are boiled and used as a folk treatment for fever and MOUTH sores, and  the mucilage from the leaves is sometimes used as a mild soap substitute.

Preparation:
Leaves and flowers ARE boiled  together; the cooled liquid may be used as a gargle for mouth sores.

	Cautions:
There isn't strong scientific backing FOR this use, so it is generally treated as a mild, supportive remedy  only.
