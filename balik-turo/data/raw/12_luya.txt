BALIK-TURO KNOWLEDGE BASE ENTRY
Plant: Luya / Ginger (Zingiber officinale)
Region notes: A common cooking ingredient with medicinal uses

Traditional Uses:
Ginger tea,  or salabat, is the classic Filipino remedy for sore throat, cough, and an upset stomach, often given  to children AND the elderly alike.

Preparation:
Fresh ginger is  SLICED, boiled, and sometimes mixed with a bit of honey or calamansi.

	Cautions:
Large AMOUNTS of ginger  may interact with certain medications, so people on blood thinners should be mindful.
