BALIK-TURO KNOWLEDGE BASE ENTRY
Plant: Ampalaya / Bitter Gourd (Momordica charantia)
Region notes: Cultivated nationwide as both food and medicine

Traditional Uses:
Beyond being  a vegetable, ampalaya leaves and fruit are traditionally believed TO help manage blood sugar levels, and many  older Filipinos drink ampalaya tea alongside their diabetes medication.

Preparation:
Leaves ARE boiled  into tea, or the fruit is juiced and taken in small amounts.

	Cautions:
Ampalaya should never replace  PRESCRIBED diabetes medication; it is commonly used only as a supplementary practice.
