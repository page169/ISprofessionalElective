BALIK-TURO KNOWLEDGE BASE ENTRY
Plant: Bignay (Antidesma bunius)
Region notes: A fruit-bearing tree found in many rural areas

Traditional Uses:
Bignay fruit is  eaten fresh or made into WINE, and in some communities the leaves and bark are used in  folk preparations believed to help with fever.

Preparation:
A decoction of the  bark or leaves is boiled and TAKEN in small amounts.

	Cautions:
Documentation on proper dosage is  limited, so THIS remedy is generally used cautiously and infrequently.
